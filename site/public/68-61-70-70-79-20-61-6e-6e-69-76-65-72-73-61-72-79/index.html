The flowers

Every petal has a quadratic Koch, or Minkowski, edge. One straight segment becomes eight smaller segments, each one quarter as long. Repeating that rule creates the intricate border; the drawing gently rounds its corners.

The leaves

The greenery is painted with “the hat”: one shape with 13 sides, assembled from eight kites. Copies can cover the plane without gaps or overlaps, but no complete tiling repeats by translation. This is called an aperiodic monotile. Hat tilings use both the original shape and its mirror image.
